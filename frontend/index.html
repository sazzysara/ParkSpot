Enterprise Capabilities

Built for Modern Residential Societies

A purpose-built suite of tools engineered for gate security guards and residential associations.

1. Smart Parking Slot Management

Visual inventory of all society parking bays. Prevents double-booking by enforcing atomic slot locking in the database.

2. Visitor Registration

Log guest vehicle plates, visitor names, and visited apartment numbers with strict Jakarta Bean Validation.

3. Vehicle Entry & Exit Tracking

Accurate entry timestamps upon arrival. Instant release upon checkout making the bay immediately available for reuse.

4. Real-Time Parking Availability

Dynamic occupancy percentages and color-coded status badges keep guards informed without manual yard inspections.

5. Society Flat Management

Centralized database linking every visiting car directly to resident flat numbers, owner names, and contact records.

6. Visitor History & Daily Reports

Filter security audit reports by calendar date. Easily export logs or review vehicle stay durations for society auditing.

Operational Workflow

How It Works at the Gate

A streamlined 5-step process ensuring zero parking conflicts and complete accountability.

1
Register Visitor

Security guard enters guest vehicle plate and selects the host flat number.

2
Record Entry

Guard picks an available bay from the dynamic dropdown and confirms allocation.

3
Track Occupancy

Slot status updates to Occupied across the system to prevent double allocation.

4
Record Vehicle Exit

When leaving, guard clicks Exit, timestamping departure and freeing the bay immediately.

5
Daily Reports

Facility managers generate daily visitor logs for verification and security audit.
